SEC. 01

A festival that keeps upgrading itself

Techfest started in 1998 as a platform for Indian students to build and showcase real projects. It has since grown into Asia's largest science and technology festival, drawing participants from thousands of colleges and universities across India and beyond, with support from patrons including UNESCO and UNICEF.

This edition takes that history and points it at one theme: augmentation. Every stage of the festival — the competitions, the exhibits, the lectures — looks at where biological limits end and engineered ones begin. Robotics that move like muscle. Interfaces that read a signal before a hand does. Machines built to finish what a body can't.

Three days on campus. Open to students, researchers, sponsors, and anyone curious enough to show up.
